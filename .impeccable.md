## Design Context

### Users
Staff and owners at a pool hall (Shy Lounge). Used operationally throughout a shift — on a tablet (hand-held, not mounted), iPhone, or laptop. Context is a dimly lit pool hall, often late at night with music and ambient noise. The interface needs to be instantly readable and tappable at a glance without demanding full attention.

### Brand Personality
**Cool. Relaxed. Late-night. Fast.**

Not "enterprise software." Not generic SaaS. The vibe is the pool hall itself — low light, felt green, the satisfying crack of a break shot. Unhurried confidence. The UI should feel like it belongs in the room.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Dark theme** — mandatory. This runs in a dimly lit space; a bright UI would be jarring.
- **Deep navy + cyan/teal accent** — already established. Keep it. The cyan reads as the pool table felt under fluorescent light.
- **Motion over stasis** — avoid anything that feels frozen or inert. Subtle, purposeful animation conveys that the app is alive (especially the live session timer).
- **No inspiration boards, no references** — original aesthetic. The goal is for it to feel inevitable for its context, not derivative.
- **Anti-references**: generic SaaS admin dashboards, over-designed "productivity apps," anything with aggressive hero metrics or identical card grids.

### Design Principles
1. **Operational clarity first** — the active table state (live timer, amount owed) is the most important information. It must be immediately legible without zooming or reading carefully.
2. **Touch-first, not just touch-friendly** — targets should feel generous; interactions should give physical feedback (active states, scale).
3. **Motion as feedback, not decoration** — the live ping, the timer tick, the loading skeleton — all motion should convey real state, not just look cool.
4. **Consistent token usage** — hardcoded hex values are a maintenance hazard and break the cohesion of the color system. Every color should come from the design token set.
5. **Between MVP and flagship** — the UI bar is high. Polish until it feels intentional in every detail, but don't gold-plate.
